# About AdventureLog

AdventureLog is an open-source travel companion for remembering where you've been and planning where you're going next. Save the locations you visit with photos, notes, and ratings, build day-by-day trips with flights and lodging, and watch the countries you've explored fill in on the world map.

<AppShot name="locations" />

## What it does

- **Remember**: save locations with visits, photos, notes, ratings, trails, and activities. See [Locations and Visits](../usage/locations.md).
- **Plan**: build day-by-day trips with flights, lodging, notes, and checklists, together with others. See [Trips and Collections](../usage/trips.md).
- **Map**: see every location on one map, and track the countries, regions, and cities you've visited. See [The Map](../usage/map.md) and [World Travel](../usage/world_travel.md).
- **Share**: invite people to a trip, or publish it as a travel story. See [Sharing and Privacy](../usage/sharing.md).

## Who it's for

- **Travelers** who want one place for their trips and memories, with no subscription. Try the [live demo](https://demo.adventurelog.app) first.
- **Self-hosters** who run their own services. AdventureLog runs in Docker on a homelab, NAS, VPS, or Kubernetes, and connects to Immich, Strava, Wanderer, Endurain, and OpenID Connect providers.
- **Families and groups**, sharing one server. Each person has their own account and decides what to share.

## Open source

AdventureLog is free under the [GPL-3.0 license](https://github.com/seanmorley15/AdventureLog/blob/main/LICENSE). It's built with SvelteKit, Django, and PostgreSQL with PostGIS.
