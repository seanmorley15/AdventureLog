# AdventureLog v0.13.1 — Cadillac

Released 10-6-2026

Hi everyone!

I'm excited to announce **AdventureLog v0.13.1 — Cadillac**, a follow-up to Acadia focused on logging places cleanly, managing your account with confidence, and keeping dates, exports, and Cloud billing consistent. This update adds duplicate-place detection, a preferred date format, faster visit logging, richer collection ZIP transfers, a rebuilt settings and billing experience, and a frontend refresh on **Svelte 5**, **DaisyUI 5**, and **Tailwind CSS 4**.

## 📍 Smarter Location Logging

- **Duplicate detection** while adding a place, matched by similar name, address, and nearby coordinates
- A warning asks **Did you mean …?** and offers **Open existing**, with the distance shown in your measurement system
- **Add Today** records a visit for the current day in one click
- **Manage Visits** from location cards and collection views, opening the editor on the visits step
- Categories created during quick-add are saved with the new location
- **Fetch Description** can pull a place summary from **Google Maps** when that integration is enabled, alongside Wikipedia

## 📅 Date and Time Preferences

- New **preferred date format** in Settings: browser default, MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, or YYYY-MM-DD
- Clock times follow that choice: 12-hour for browser default and MM/DD/YYYY, 24-hour for DD/MM/YYYY and YYYY-MM-DD
- Shared **date and time picker** across visits, lodging, transport, and other dated fields
- Calendar and the rest of the app display dates with the same preference

## 📄 Collection Export and Import

- Collection **ZIP export** includes trip start and end dates
- Lodging export includes check-in, check-out, timezone, reservation number, price, and coordinates
- Transportation export includes the end time and origin and destination coordinates
- Notes and checklists round-trip with their dates and items
- Older ZIP files that used previous field names still import
- Collection **PDF** export splits long notes and descriptions across pages so large itineraries stay printable

## 👤 Accounts, Security, and Settings

- Settings is organized into **Profile**, **Emails**, **Security**, **Integrations**, **Data & Storage**, **Danger Zone**, **About**, and **Administration** for staff
- Sign in with **username or email**
- Password rules are shown on signup, password change, and reset
- `ACCOUNT_PASSWORD_MIN_LENGTH` sets the minimum length (default 6)
- `AUTH_PASSWORD_VALIDATORS_ENABLED` can also reject common, all-numeric, and profile-similar passwords
- When `TERMS_OF_SERVICE_URL` or `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` is set, signup asks you to accept the linked policies
- **Active sessions**: see where you are signed in and sign out other devices
- Password confirmation before turning multi-factor authentication on or off
- **Delete account** removes locations, collections, media, and integrations, and cancels an active AdventureLog Cloud subscription
- Staff accounts stay in place until an administrator removes staff status
- Invitation links open a dedicated signup flow for the invited email, with clear messages for expired, used, or invalid invites
- Social sign-in failures land on a page that explains what happened
- **Media storage** usage is shown in settings

## ☁️ AdventureLog Cloud Billing

- Rebuilt **Billing & Subscription** page with trial, active, past due, canceled, and scheduled states (#1250)
- Trial countdown, renewal date, and media storage on one page
- **Manage billing** opens the Stripe portal for your payment method, invoices, and cancellation
- A banner appears when Cloud access is paused or a trial is still running
- Self-hosted instances explain that every feature is available without a Cloud subscription

## 🗺️ Maps and World Data

- Map drawing stays stable while the map is resized
- Regions missing their own coordinates use the **country centroid** so they can still be placed
- CARTO basemaps accept `PUBLIC_CARTO_BASEMAP_API_KEY` for domain-restricted tile requests (#1277)
- Bundled country and region data updated to **v3.2**
- Photo location pins stay on the map when an image or its coordinates are missing
- Card menus flip to stay on screen, with clearer roles for assistive technology

## 🎨 Frontend Foundation

- Frontend migrated to **Svelte 5**
- UI kit updated to **DaisyUI 5** and **Tailwind CSS 4**
- Location editing avoids reactive update loops that could stall the editor
- The timezone selector starts from the browser timezone

## 🛠️ Operations and Community

- **Easypanel** deployment guide for the one-click AdventureLog template (#1283)
- Startup checks require `SITE_URL` to begin with `http://` or `https://`
- Added a **Code of Conduct** and **Security Policy**, and refreshed contribution guidelines and issue templates

## 🌐 Languages

- New **Alemannic**, **Indonesian**, and **Luxembourgish** locales
- Weblate updates across Arabic, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified), Czech, Dutch, French, German, Hungarian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian Bokmål, Polish, Portuguese, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish, and Ukrainian

## ✨ Miscellaneous

- Itinerary items keep a stable content type so each day retains its locations, lodging, transport, notes, and checklists
- Broader automated tests for auth, invites, sessions, collection ZIP transfer, duplicates, and billing webhooks
- General UI polish and backend error-handling refinements
