# AdventureLog v0.14.0 "Abisko"

Released TBD

Hi everyone!

I'm excited to announce **AdventureLog v0.14.0 "Abisko"**, the biggest update to trip planning since collections were introduced. Trips are now **planned day by day**, sharing is rebuilt around **roles, guest links, and published travel stories**, and your travel companions can **comment and follow along** as the plan takes shape. This release also brings a printable **Trip Book**, a redesigned home and detail pages, background jobs, lower memory use, and a cleaner, documented API.

> [!WARNING]
> **Upgrading to v0.14.0 is strongly recommended.** This release fixes several privately reported security issues. Please read the **Upgrade Notes** below before updating, and back up your data first, as always.

## 🗓️ Trips, Planned Day by Day

- Collections now have a **type**: a **Trip** plans an itinerary day by day, and a **Folder** simply groups places without needing dates
- Plan a trip by its **dates**, or by a **number of days** and set the start date later
- Every trip has **Ideas** for places you might fit in, and **Trip-wide** notes and checklists for the whole journey
- **Drag and drop** stops within a day, onto another day, or back to Ideas (#1079), with a short press on touch screens so scrolling never drags by accident (#1089)
- Lodging and transport can **span several days**, shown as "Day 2 → 4", and a stop can sit on more than one day
- Mark stops as **optional** and give them **start and end times**
- **Day actions**: insert a day before or after, duplicate it, move it earlier or later, or delete it, and choose where its stops go
- **Shift dates** moves the whole trip: days, order, and clock times stay put, lodging and transport move with their days, and daylight saving time is handled
- **Log visit** or **Log day** straight from the plan when you have been somewhere
- Attach **activities, trails, GPX tracks, and photos to a day**, even on hikes where there's no single place to anchor them (#1074)
- **Connect stops** draws a line through a day's stops in order on the map
- **Jump to a day**, copy a **link to a specific day**, and open a running trip on **today** (#1346)
- New visits, stays, and journeys start in the **trip's time zone** (#653, #801)
- **Add existing** places from your account, or browse **places near this collection**
- Older date-based trips keep working, and **Plan by days** converts them in one click
- When someone else changes the plan while you're editing, you see the latest version instead of overwriting it

## 🤝 Sharing, Rebuilt

- A new **Share** sheet brings people, links, and visibility together in one place
- Invite people as an **Editor**, who can change days, places, notes, bookings, and files, or a **Viewer**, who can read the whole plan (#919)
- **Guest links** open the private plan without an account, with a label, an optional expiry, a role, and a one-click revoke (#597)
- Choose who can read the published story: **Private**, **Everyone signed in** (#621), **Unlisted**, or **Public**
- **Save & share** right from the new collection form (#967)
- Edit a collection from its own page (#1147)
- Anyone who can read a place can **copy it into their own account** (#1005)
- Photos and files on lodging, transport, and notes follow the collection's sharing, so public collections show them correctly (#952)

## 📖 Travel Stories

- Publish any collection as a **travel story**: a read-only page with its days, map, places, photos, and bookings, made to share with friends and family
- **You choose what the story shows.** Costs (#1043), reservation numbers, exact lodging addresses, map pins, attachments, GPX tracks, note text, and checklist items each stay hidden until you turn them on
- Hide any single note from the published story, even when note text is shown
- **Preview** exactly what readers see before you share the link

## 💬 Comments and Activity

- **Plan comments** for everyone on the trip, with replies, **@mentions**, and email notifications for mentions
- **Story comments** let readers react to a published story, open to signed-in readers or to guest links too (#1297)
- Filter comments by day, and catch up in the **New comments** inbox
- An **Activity** feed shows who moved what, who joined, and when the trip dates changed

## 🔐 Privacy Controls

- Profiles are **Private**, visible to **Signed-in users**, or **Public** (#1226). Signed-in profiles can be found and invited to collections without being public
- Server administrators can set **`ALLOW_PUBLIC_ACCESS=False`** to keep everything behind sign-in: public and unlisted stories, public places, profiles, guest links, and their files (#1124). See [Public Access](/docs/configuration/public_access)

## 📄 Trip Book and Field Guide

- Collection PDFs are rebuilt from the ground up
- Trips export a **Trip Book** to travel with, even offline: a cover, an overview map, bookings and costs, and a page for every day
- Folders export a **Field Guide**: a workbook with an overview map, a checklist, and an entry for each place
- Choose **A4 or Letter**, include or leave out **photos** (#1299), and add **space to write** for a travel journal
- **Maps** are drawn from OpenStreetMap when the server can reach it
- Long notes and descriptions flow across pages, so large trips always export (#1288)
- Exports respect what each reader is allowed to see

## 📍 Places, Stays, and Journeys

- Redesigned **detail pages** for places, lodging, and transport, with photos, files, an overview, and quick actions to copy coordinates or a map link
- **Open in Immich** jumps to a place on your Immich map, next to Apple Maps, Google Maps, and OpenStreetMap (#1117)
- The Immich photo picker opens on the **day a place is planned for** (#1255), and saving the Immich settings keeps your existing API key
- **Motorcycle** is a new transportation type (#1052)
- Prices and your default currency now offer **every currency in circulation**, named in your language (#1014)
- Filter a collection's places by **minimum rating** (#1234)
- Collection import can **use places you already have** instead of creating duplicates
- New card designs show countdowns, "Day 3 of 7", visit counts, and checklist progress at a glance

## 🧭 Recommendations

- Find places **near your trip** by category: attractions, arts and culture, nature and parks, cafés and nightlife, and activities and shopping
- Search near a city or the current map area, sort by best match, distance, or rating, and **add to a day or to Ideas** in one tap

## 📅 Calendar

- Your calendar shows the **stops and activities planned on trip days**
- A flight that departs at midnight UTC (8 PM in New York) stays a timed event instead of turning into an all-day one (#1327)
- Every transportation type shows its own icon

## 🗺️ Maps and Geocoding

- **OpenStreetMap results in your language** (#957)
- Places in **Gibraltar** (#1118), **Åland** (#1212), **Sint Maarten**, and other territories land in the right country and region
- New management commands fix places saved with the wrong country: `python manage.py fix_location_countries` and `python manage.py reassign_territory_locations`
- Bundled country and region data updated to **v5.2.0**
- A transportation with a GPX track no longer also shows a straight line (#1318)
- Show or hide **photo location pins** on the map
- Geocoding runs once per place, in the background, so new places never fail to get a city, region, or country (#1262)

## 🔌 Integrations

- **Endurain v0.20** support (#1351), and the activity list no longer breaks on missing metrics (#1311)
- **Wanderer** trail durations display correctly (#956)
- More reliable **Strava** connection checks
- New `INTEGRATIONS_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NETWORKS` setting: integrations on your LAN keep working by default, and instances open to the public can turn it off so no user can reach the server's own network

## 🔒 Security

This release fixes several privately reported security issues. Thank you to the researchers who reported them responsibly; advisories will be published once the release is out.

- Tighter permission checks across itineraries, notes, shared media, and integration requests
- Notes and descriptions render **safe Markdown only**: raw HTML is shown as text
- **Social sign-in** only matches an existing account by a **verified** email address; accounts that already signed in this way keep working
- API keys stop working as soon as their user is deactivated
- A weak or missing `DJANGO_ADMIN_PASSWORD` is replaced by a generated password on first start
- Requests to integrations go through a hardened client that refuses cloud metadata addresses

## ⚙️ Self-Hosting and Performance

- **Background jobs**: geocoding, backups, collection archives, PDFs, restores, and imports run in a worker inside the same container, with no extra services. Large backups restore without timeouts (#1133). See [Background Jobs](/docs/configuration/background_jobs)
- The old cron jobs are replaced by built-in scheduled tasks
- **Lower memory use**: an idle all-in-one container uses roughly 40% less RAM
- **Caching** with the bundled memcached by default, or Redis with `CACHE_BACKEND=redis`. See [Caching](/docs/configuration/caching)
- `BODY_SIZE_LIMIT` now also sets the upload limit of the bundled nginx
- Tuned Gunicorn settings, with `GUNICORN_THREADS` to adjust them

## 🧑‍💻 For Developers and API Users

- **The API is reorganised and documented.** Every endpoint lives under `/api/`, browser sign-in under `/api/auth/browser/v1/`, and interactive docs are served at **`/api/docs/`** with the schema at `/api/schema/`
- Errors always use one format: `{ "detail", "code", "fields" }`
- **Conditional writes** (`If-Match`) and **client-chosen IDs** make offline and multi-device editing safe
- Sign-in for **mobile apps** with social providers and session tokens
- API key checks are now fast, instead of taking around 300 ms per request (#1304)
- Places can be created and updated with their visits in one request (#888, #915, #1306), checklist items keep their IDs when saved (#1305), and categories are found by their display name (#1307)
- New frontend test suite with **Vitest** unit tests and **Playwright** end-to-end journeys (#643), plus a much larger backend suite

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- The **Admin Panel** link in the avatar menu no longer shows a 404 (#1376)
- Editing a flight from the itinerary no longer fails with a server error (#1384)
- Lodging saves from the itinerary dialog (#1104, #1069)
- Collections with large GPX tracks load again (#1164)
- Every option in an item's menu is reachable on the last day of a trip (#1177)
- Itinerary items link to their full details: links, files, and notes (#965)
- Changing a collection's dates keeps its Trip Context items (#1328)
- Saving a checklist keeps its items, and ticking one box no longer recreates the list (#1305)
- First-boot admin creation works with any password, including quotes (#1090)
- Creating a user in the Django admin without an email works (#1267)
- Visits saved in UTC keep their time zone, the locations list refreshes after edits, and the navbar no longer clips

## ⬆️ Upgrade Notes

- **Back up first**, then update as usual. Database migrations run automatically when the container starts
- **API clients:** endpoints moved under `/api/` and error responses changed. The old-to-new route table is in `backend/server/API.md`, and the full reference is at `/api/docs/` on your server
- **Admin password:** if `DJANGO_ADMIN_PASSWORD` is empty or weak (such as `admin`), a new one is generated on first start and printed once: `docker compose logs app | grep -A3 "Admin username"`
- **Social sign-in:** linking to an existing account now requires a verified email. Accounts that signed in this way before keep working
- **Notes:** raw HTML in notes and descriptions now shows as text. Markdown formatting is unchanged
- **Private networks:** if strangers can sign up to your instance, set `INTEGRATIONS_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NETWORKS=False`
- **New optional settings:** `ALLOW_PUBLIC_ACCESS`, `CACHE_BACKEND`, `REDIS_URL`, `GUNICORN_THREADS`, and `INTEGRATIONS_ALLOW_PRIVATE_NETWORKS`. See [Environment Variables](/docs/configuration/environment_variables)
